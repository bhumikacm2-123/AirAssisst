
# Indian City Air Quality Guide

## 1. Introduction

Air quality can vary between cities depending on
traffic, industries, weather, construction and other factors.

AirAssist uses air-quality data to help users understand
pollution levels in Indian cities.

## 2. Major Factors Affecting City Air Quality

### Vehicle Traffic
Cars, buses, trucks and two-wheelers release pollutants
through fuel combustion.

### Industrial Activities
Factories and industrial facilities can release gases
and particulate matter.

### Construction and Road Dust
Construction, demolition and road dust can contribute
to PM10 and PM2.5 pollution.

### Waste Burning
Open burning of waste releases harmful particles
and gases.

### Weather Conditions
Wind, rainfall, temperature and atmospheric conditions
can influence how pollutants spread or accumulate.

## 3. Understanding City Comparisons

When comparing air quality between cities:

- Compare the same pollutant.
- Check that measurements use the same units.
- Compare measurements from similar dates and periods.
- Consider the number of monitoring stations.
- Check for missing observations.
- Do not assume one monitoring station represents
  the entire city.

## 4. Understanding Monitoring Stations

Air-quality monitoring stations measure pollutant
concentrations at particular locations.

Readings can differ between stations because of
nearby traffic, industries and local conditions.

## 5. How AirAssist Uses City Data

AirAssist can use city-level datasets to:

- Display pollutant concentrations.
- Compare cities using available measurements.
- Show historical pollution patterns.
- Display monitoring-station information.
- Support PM10 model experiments.

## 6. Important Data Limitations

A single measurement is not enough to describe
a city's long-term air quality.

Historical observations should not be presented
as current real-time readings.

Missing values and limited station coverage can
affect comparisons.

## 7. Important Disclaimer

Air quality changes over time.

Always check current official monitoring data
and government advisories for the latest conditions.
