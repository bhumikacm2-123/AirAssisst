
# AirAssist - Air Quality Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. What is air quality?

Air quality describes how clean or polluted the air is
and whether it may affect human health.

## 2. What is AQI?

AQI stands for Air Quality Index.
It is a numerical indicator used to communicate
air pollution levels and associated health impacts.

## 3. What is a good AQI?

In India's AQI system, a value between 0 and 50
is classified as Good.

## 4. What does AQI 150 mean?

In India's AQI system, 150 falls under the Moderate
category (101-200).

Sensitive people may experience breathing discomfort.

## 5. What does AQI 250 mean?

A value of 250 falls under the Poor category
(201-300) in India's AQI system.

Prolonged exposure may cause breathing discomfort.

## 6. Which pollutant is most harmful?

There is no single pollutant that is always the most harmful.
Health impact depends on concentration, exposure duration
and individual vulnerability.

## 7. What is the difference between PM2.5 and PM10?

PM2.5 particles are 2.5 micrometres or smaller.

PM10 particles are 10 micrometres or smaller.

PM2.5 can penetrate deeper into the lungs.

## 8. How can I protect myself from air pollution?

- Check the local AQI.
- Follow official health advisories.
- Reduce prolonged outdoor exertion when pollution is high.
- Use a well-fitted particulate respirator when appropriate.
- Seek medical advice for persistent symptoms.

## 9. Does rain improve air quality?

Rain can temporarily remove some particles from the air.
However, the effect varies with weather and local pollution sources.

## 10. Why does air quality change during the day?

Air quality can change due to traffic, industrial activity,
wind, temperature and atmospheric conditions.

## 11. Can indoor air also be polluted?

Yes. Indoor sources can include cooking smoke,
tobacco smoke, dust and some household products.

## 12. Is AQI the same as PM2.5?

No. AQI is an index.
PM2.5 is one pollutant that can contribute to the AQI.

## 13. Can AirAssist provide medical advice?

AirAssist provides general educational health guidance.
It does not diagnose diseases or replace a doctor.

## 14. Where can I check official Indian AQI data?

Use the Central Pollution Control Board's official portal:

https://airquality.cpcb.gov.in/AQI_India/

## 15. Important Note

Air quality information should be interpreted using
official monitoring data.

Always check the date, location and pollutant units
before making decisions.
