CLINICAL PUBLIC HEALTH KNOWLEDGE

Air Pollutant Toxicology & Respiratory Protection Protocols

Scientific guidelines synthesized from the World Health Organization (WHO), ICMR, and AIIMS Delhi.

Certified Mask Standards

N95 / FFP2 / BIS 9473: Captures ≥95% of 0.3-micron particles with an airtight nasal clip seal. Cloth and standard surgical masks leak over 60% of PM2.5 and offer negligible protection against combustion soot.

Indoor Air Purification

True HEPA (H13 grade): Mechanically removes 99.97% of fine particulates. Select an air purifier matching your room volume. Avoid uncertified ionizers that release secondary ozone ($O_3$).

PM2.5 vs. PM10 Dynamics

PM2.5: Bypasses nasal cilia, penetrates deep into alveolar capillaries, and promotes cardiac inflammation. PM10: Irritates upper nasopharynx, causing acute coughing and sinus headaches.

Emergency SOS Protocol

If experiencing cyanosis (blue lips/fingers), severe acute chest tightness, or oxygen saturation dropping below 90%, immediately dial national emergency ambulance services: 108 / 112.
